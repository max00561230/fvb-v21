## Key Rules
- Historical page images are the permanent source of truth
- Never generate, redraw, or replace faces or photographs
- Never reconstruct pages from HTML text blocks
- All features are overlays/panels — never alter the page image
- Page 6 is permanently locked (James Henry Francis & Pearl Martha Ann Clanton Francis)
- When uncertain, "No Change" is always preferred over an incorrect change

## AI Behavior Contract
